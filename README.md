# Best Selling Car Brands in India

## Introduction
This project analyzes the **Best Selling Car Brands in India**, focusing on the top-selling brands in the second-hand car market. The goal is to provide insights into the sales performance of different car brands, highlight trends in the Indian car market, and offer recommendations for both buyers and sellers.

## Features
- **Brand Sales Performance**: Analyze the total sales performance of leading car brands in India.
- **Price Analysis**: Explore the price range and pricing trends for the most popular car models.
- **Mileage Comparison**: Compare the mileage of different car brands and models.
- **Market Share Analysis**: Understand the market share of the leading car brands in India’s second-hand car market.
- **Car Condition Analysis**: Assess how the condition of the car impacts its sales and price.

## Insights

- **Market Leadership of Maruti Suzuki**:  
  **Maruti Suzuki** is the top-selling brand in the second-hand car market in India, with the highest **sales volume** and **market share**. The brand’s popularity is driven by its affordability, reliability, and wide availability in the market.

- **BMW - Luxury Brand with Strong Position**:  
  **BMW** ranks **third** in the second-hand car market in India. As a **luxury brand**, it continues to have a strong demand despite being positioned in a higher price segment. This reflects the growing popularity of premium cars in the second-hand market.

- **BMW's Younger Vehicles**:  
  **BMW** vehicles in the second-hand market are generally **younger** compared to other brands, with the **lowest average vehicle age**. This indicates that buyers prefer newer models from luxury brands, which offer the latest features and better condition.

- **Fuel Type Preference**:  
  **Petrol cars** dominate the second-hand market, with **higher sales** compared to **diesel** vehicles. Conversely, **LPG**, **CNG**, and **electric vehicles** make up a very small fraction of the market, indicating that alternative fuel cars are not as popular in the second-hand segment.

- **Top-Selling Maruti Dzire and Manual Transmission**:  
  Among **Maruti Suzuki** models, the **Maruti Dzire** stands out as the **top-selling car** in the second-hand market. Additionally, **manual transmission** cars, like the Dzire, continue to sell better compared to **automatic transmission** vehicles, indicating that manual cars are more in demand in the second-hand market.


## Recommendations

- **Focus on Affordable Models Like Dzire**:  
  Since **Maruti Suzuki** leads the second-hand car market with models like the **Maruti Dzire**, it's recommended for dealerships and sellers to **focus on stocking affordable and popular models**. Given the demand for **manual transmission cars**, emphasizing these options can drive sales.

- **Target the Growing Demand for Petrol Cars**:  
  With **petrol cars** dominating the market, it's advised to **prioritize petrol variants** in the second-hand inventory. While **diesel** vehicles have some demand, the focus on petrol cars will likely yield better sales, considering consumer preferences.

- **Capitalizing on Luxury Segment with Younger Vehicles**:  
  For **BMW** and other luxury brands, dealerships should **emphasize newer models** that attract buyers looking for premium features and better condition. By focusing on **relatively younger** vehicles in the second-hand market, luxury brands can appeal to customers seeking the latest technology and design.

- **Promote Manual Transmission Cars**:  
  Given that **manual transmission cars** are selling better than **automatic transmission** cars, it's advisable for sellers to **prioritize manual variants** in their listings. This trend may reflect local driving preferences, so aligning the inventory accordingly can boost sales.
